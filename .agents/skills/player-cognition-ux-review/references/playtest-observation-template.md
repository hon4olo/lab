## Instructions to tester

Do not teach the game unless the test is blocked. Ask the player to think aloud. Observe what they do, not only what they say.

## Post-test questions

Ask after play, not during:

1. What was your goal?
2. What was the most confusing moment?
3. What felt good?
4. What felt unfair or annoying?
5. What would you try next if you kept playing?
6. What do you think the main strategy is?
