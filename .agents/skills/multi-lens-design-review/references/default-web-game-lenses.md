# Default Web Game Lenses

Use this lens set for CrazyGames-style browser games.

## 1. Essential Experience

- What should the player feel in the first 30 seconds?
- What is the one moment the game is built around?
- Are features supporting or diluting that moment?

## 2. Player

- Who is likely to click this game?
- What genre knowledge do they have?
- What will they misunderstand?
- How impatient are they?

## 3. Toy

- Is the core action fun by itself?
- Can the player play with it before goals are introduced?
- Does the toy produce surprise or mastery?

## 4. Core Loop

- What is the smallest repeatable loop?
- How fast does it complete?
- What creates the next desire?

## 5. Goals

- Is the next goal obvious?
- Is the goal meaningful?
- Can the player act toward it immediately?

## 6. Meaningful Choice

- What choice changes future play?
- Are options meaningfully different?
- Are consequences predictable enough?

## 7. Challenge / Flow

- Is the player learning or merely surviving?
- Does difficulty follow mastery?
- Does failure teach?

## 8. Skill vs Chance

- Is randomness exciting or unfair?
- Can skill reduce risk?
- Is the chance/skill ratio right for the audience?

## 9. Reward / Punishment

- Are rewards immediate and useful?
- Does punishment teach without causing churn?
- Are rewards aligned with desired behavior?

## 10. Simplicity / Complexity

- What can be cut from the first session?
- Which systems should be delayed?
- Is complexity creating depth or friction?

## 11. Balance

- Is there a dominant strategy?
- Are weak options secretly traps?
- Are costs and rewards proportional?

## 12. Interface / Feedback

- Can the player see what matters?
- Does every action respond?
- Does UI help or interrupt?

## 13. Juiciness

- Which action needs stronger sensory feedback?
- Which effect is noise?
- Does juice clarify the game state?

## 14. Interest Curve

- Where are the peaks?
- Is the first peak early enough?
- What novelty arrives before boredom?

## 15. Surprise / Curiosity

- What is the player curious about?
- What is revealed gradually?
- Are surprises earned and understandable?

## 16. Theme / Resonance

- Does the theme strengthen mechanics?
- Is the fantasy coherent?
- Do art/audio/UI/mechanics point to the same feeling?

## 17. Playtesting

- What is the riskiest assumption?
- Who can reveal the truth?
- What behavior will prove success?

## 18. Technology

- Is tech risk justified?
- Does performance support feel?
- Is this feasible for browser/mobile?

## 19. Profit / Responsibility

- How does the design sustain itself?
- Does monetization respect the player?
- Does the game avoid dark patterns?
