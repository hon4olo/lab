# Title and Description Template

## Title

**Two to four words.** Longer titles truncate in grids, and you do not control where.

| Rule | Why |
|---|---|
| Front-load the distinctive word | If it truncates, what survives should still identify the game |
| Genre or verb inferable | "Rooftop Dash" tells you more than "Aetheria" |
| Searchable over clever | Players search for what they want to do |
| No collision with an established game | You will lose that search result forever |
| Readable by a non-native speaker | Portal traffic is global |

Test: show the title alone to five people and ask what kind of game it is. If they cannot guess the genre, the title is doing no work.

## Short description

Portal listings truncate hard and compete with the play button. Three sentences, in this order:

1. **Fantasy and verb.** The only sentence most people read.
2. **Loop and reward.** What you do repeatedly and what you get.
3. **Hook.** The twist, mode or variety that makes it not the same as the others.

### Template

```txt
[Verb] your way through [setting] as [role/fantasy].
[Core loop] to earn [reward] and unlock [progression].
[Distinctive hook — mode, twist, or variety].
```

### Example

```txt
Sprint across collapsing rooftops as a courier who cannot stop.
Chain jumps to build speed, bank coins, and unlock faster gear.
Every run reshuffles the skyline, so no two routes are the same.
```

### Do not include

- Studio history or "we're a small indie team"
- "We hope you enjoy"
- Feature bullet lists
- Control instructions — those belong in the game
- Superlatives: "the best", "addictive", "amazing"

## Tags

- Match what the game actually is. Mis-tagged traffic bounces, and bounce rate feeds portal ranking.
- Primary genre first and unambiguous.
- Do not tag a genre you merely gesture at. A runner with three upgrades is not an RPG.
- Check what tags the top games in your category use, and be honest about which of them apply.

## Screenshot order

| Slot | Job |
|---|---|
| 1 | Core verb, mid-action |
| 2 | Reward or progression |
| 3 | Challenge or conflict |
| 4 | Variety — a different biome, mode or enemy |

If two screenshots look alike at a glance, one is wasted.
