# Thumbnail Checklist

The thumbnail competes in a grid against ~40 others, at small size, against a scrolling player. It gets about half a second.

## Test conditions

Review it the way it will be seen, not the way you made it:

1. Export at 128×128 and at the portal's actual grid size.
2. Place it in a grid of ten real competitor thumbnails.
3. Look for two seconds. Which one draws the eye?
4. Ask someone what they think the game is.

Most failed thumbnails were only ever reviewed at 100% zoom, alone.

## Checklist

- [ ] **One subject.** Two focal points read as noise at small size.
- [ ] **The subject is doing the core verb.** Mid-jump, mid-drift, mid-swing — not standing still.
- [ ] **Legible silhouette.** Squint until it blurs; the shape should still be identifiable.
- [ ] **High contrast** between subject and background.
- [ ] **A face or front-facing subject** if the game has one. Eyes attract attention in a grid.
- [ ] **No small text.** Unreadable at size, and portals often overlay the title anyway. One word maximum.
- [ ] **Not a raw screenshot.** Gameplay framing is designed for playing, not for a 128 px cell.
- [ ] **Genre inferable** in under a second.
- [ ] **Honest.** Does not promise visuals or content the game lacks.
- [ ] **Distinct** from the neighbouring games in its category.

## Common failures

| Failure | Why it loses |
|---|---|
| Wide gameplay shot | Subject too small to read |
| Character standing idle | No action, no fantasy |
| Logo as the thumbnail | Means nothing to someone who has not heard of you |
| Dark scene | Disappears in a bright grid |
| Busy background | Silhouette illegible |
| Rear view of the character | No face, no eye contact |
| Overpromised render | High click, terrible retention, worse ranking |

## When traffic is low but retention is fine

The problem is the thumbnail, then the title, in that order. It is almost never the game. Change one, measure for a full week, then change the other.

## A/B rules

- One variable per test.
- Minimum one full week, to cover weekday and weekend traffic.
- Judge on click-through **and** post-click behaviour. A thumbnail that raises clicks and lowers play time is a lie that portal ranking will eventually punish.
