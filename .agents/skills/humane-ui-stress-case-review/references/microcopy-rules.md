# Humane Microcopy Rules

## The shape of a good message

**What happened → what it means for you → what you can do now.**

Three short clauses. Anything longer is not read at the moment it matters.

## Rules

- **Plain language.** No error codes as the primary message, no jargon, no internal terminology.
- **Be specific.** "Need 40 more coins" beats "not enough coins", because it tells the player how close they are.
- **Always give a next action.** A message with no path forward is a dead end.
- **Own system failures.** If the game failed, say "we couldn't save your progress", not "your progress was not saved". Passive voice reads as evasion.
- **Never blame the player.** "You didn't watch the whole ad" is an accusation; "The ad didn't finish, so no reward was given" is a fact.
- **No fake urgency.** No countdown that resets, no "last chance" that comes back.
- **No misleading "free".** If it costs 30 seconds of attention, say what it is.
- **State what happens if they decline.** "No thanks, restart" not "Skip".

## Never do these

- **Jokes during loss, error or payment.** Humour reads as mockery when the reader is annoyed.
- **Exclamation marks in failure states.** Enthusiasm about someone's bad moment is gloating.
- **"Oops".** It is the sound of a system that does not know what went wrong.
- **"Something went wrong".** True, useless, and the player already knew.
- **Guilt on the decline path.** "Are you sure you don't want to get stronger?" is a dark pattern in a friendly voice.
- **Celebration of small failures.** A full game-over screen after a two-second run is an insult; restart instantly instead.

## Rewriting checklist

For each string, ask:

1. Would I say this out loud to someone who just lost twenty minutes of progress?
2. Does it say what happened, or only that something happened?
3. Does it give a next step?
4. Does it blame anyone?
5. Is there a number I could include that would make it useful?

## Localisation note

Short, plain, literal strings translate well. Jokes, idioms and wordplay do not — they either break or become nonsense in half your markets. On a portal with global traffic this matters more than voice.
